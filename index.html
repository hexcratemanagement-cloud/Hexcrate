FAQ

Before you order.

Does my customer need an app?

No. Most recent iPhone and Android phones read the NFC card directly. Every card also has a QR code, so any phone camera works too.

Can I change my link later?

Yes. The card points to a link we set up for you, so you can change where it goes anytime without buying a new card.

Do the cards need charging?

No. The chip has no battery. It wakes up when a phone gets close.

Can you use my own design?

Yes. The Custom NFC Tap Card is printed with your design. Send us your logo and ideas when you order and we will confirm the artwork before printing.

How long does an order take, and do you deliver?

We confirm the timeline and delivery options with your quote, before you pay. Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram to start.

What if my card does not work?

We test every card before it reaches you. If something is wrong, message us and we will help you fix it.
